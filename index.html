Welcome to the Hedlin Family Journal

Time is easier to understand once all its pieces are set out to be looked at and talked about. Here an account of who we were, what we thought, what we talked about, what we ate and read and played at, and who were our friends and family.

Some things carry on from start to finish, some things appear briefly and vanish. We can see what is our bedrock, what is transitional, what is memorable, what we wish we had done more, or differently, or not at all.
