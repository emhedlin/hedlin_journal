We went to Pier 39 for coffee and danishes, then checked out of the motel. We drove to Sausalito. The fog was so thick the Golden Gate bridge and the water passed unseen.

In Sausalito, Cheryl, Mama and Papa walked through the shops. Peter, Erik, Matthew and I followed the shore to the end, studying crabs on rocks, past restaurants sitting on piles, then back to look at the docked boats. A Chinese junk sailed into the harbour. I pointed it out to the boys and took a picture. Erik though the name was funny.  Coming off the dock we passed a pile of chopped wood. He said, ‘Now there’s Chinese junk.’

We joined up with the others, ate lunch at a hamburger cafe, sat awhile as a long line of people built up on the sidewalk, then returned to the van. We returned along the Golden Gate bridge but missed our intended target, a children’s playground, and headed instead directly to Oakland to get our hotel room. The Days’ Inn was overbooked. They transferred us to the Clarion. We swam in the large pool, at at its new coffee shop, caught the shuttle to the Coliseum for the game between the A’s and Royals. We sat by 3rd base foul pole. Tickets were $12 each. Ricky Henderson stood very near. He turned, looked the crowd over, waved a couple of times, made plays to back up the centre fielder, caught someone out. Jose Canseco was ejected. Carney Lansford hit a grand slam, the ball landing 5 meters from us. The boys ate malts. While waiting for our hotel shuttle a scuffle broke out in the parking lot. Security arrived. We passed a car with 4 or 5 guys with their hands on a van, getting frisked.
