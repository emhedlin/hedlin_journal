Three short-haired males beating the summer heat, just when the temperature becomes more moderate, breezy, cloudy.

Peter helped Cheryl make my upcoming birthday cake—German Chocolate—by cutting out large circles in wax paper

Two days left of swimming lessons. Both boys have enjoyed the water. We don’t want to push them too quickly. Matthew starts by getting his hair wet, face in the water, blowing bubbles, singing games in circles: ‘What time is it Mister Woof?’ He will wander, which means the instructor retrieving him, returning with him under her arm. Peter is still the littlest in his class. How to get around that when he is always first in cue, following instructions to the ‘t’—head in-head out, scaling the dolphin, HELP (heat-escape-lessening-posture), which is used if one has fallen into cold water), rescue breathing (which he struggles with a bit, doing it on strangers, which is anyone who is not family). He said, ‘I could spend 5 hours in the pool.’ He’ll spend the next two weeks at the University doing an hour a day (5-6) on stroke improvement with the Goldfins.

Cheryl’s parents returned from Banff after attending Rob Ardell’s wedding in Calgary.

Cheryl is on her last legs. She cannot wait for the baby to arrive. Once can see its movements through her clothing. Matthew thinks it’s cute, rubs it carefully. Peter can’t wait for it to be born. He’s sure it’s a girl.

Matthew has a new friend. John Scott Ford. They play football together, and baseball, tennis, and birdball. He plays tricks on Matthew. He takes Matthew’s wallet, hides his shoes. His hair is dark. He is bigger than Matthew. He is entirely in Matthew’s imagination.

Peter played cello for the first time since early June.

We have just purchased a set of bunk beds. Lately they have been sleeping together and wake every morning wretched with tiredness. One night Matthew fell out of bed, taking the quilts with him. This doesn’t stop them from being up early, engaging with each other all day.

Peter put out 2 old teeth for the tooth fairy.

Prince Andrew and Sara Ferguson married today.
